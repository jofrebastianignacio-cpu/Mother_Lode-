**Minerals, alloys, gems and new gear underground.** Eight materials, a new ore, seven gems, full gear for each one and an in-game guide book that explains everything.

![All items](https://jofrebastianignacio-cpu.github.io/Mother_Lode-/img/vista_previa.png)

📖 **Full guide:** [jofrebastianignacio-cpu.github.io/Mother_Lode-](https://jofrebastianignacio-cpu.github.io/Mother_Lode-/) · *Descripción en español más abajo.*

## Features

- **8 materials** with full gear: sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe, spear, bow, crossbow, 3x3 hammer, 3x3 excavator, paxel, armor and horse armor.
- **Rutile:** a new deep ore (Y -64 to 0) that smelts into titanium in a blast furnace.
- **3 metal shields** with their own designs.
- **7 gems** with permanent effects while carried. Four of them have an on/off switch and glow while active.
- **3 foods**, **3 upgrade templates**, loot in structures, villager trades and armor trims.
- **Own advancement tab** with 23 advancements and a lush cave background.
- **Built-in Faithful 32x and 64x packs.**

## Installation

Requires **Minecraft 26.2** and **NeoForge 26.2.0.90+**. Put the jar in your `mods` folder. When updating from 1.0.0, remove the old jar first; worlds keep working.
